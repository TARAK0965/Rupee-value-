Android Notification Bridge

          
        

        

          

            Stream phone push notifications (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Bank SMS) directly into Campus Ledger in 3 ways:
          

          

            

              1. WebOTP Native SMS:
              
Tap "Start Listening for Phone SMS" in Slide 1. When an SMS arrives, Android asks permission and pipes it straight in.

            

            

              2. 1-Tap Clipboard:
              
Whenever you copy a transaction message or OTP, click "Read Copied SMS".

            

            

              3. MacroDroid / Tasker Webhook:
              
Send an HTTP POST to: /api/incoming-notification with JSON {"text": "your SMS text"}.
